# 0007. Homepage visibility, Resources ordering, and deferred legal pages

Date: 2026-10-22
Status: Accepted

## Context
The PRD and Resources review had four open product questions: whether Programming belongs on the homepage, how papers should be ordered, whether Resources should remember a student's year, and whether to create legal pages now.

## Decision
- Keep Programming hidden from the homepage and its tool navigation.
- Order Resources papers by exam sequence: MST 1, MST 2, then EST.
- Do not remember the student's year or personalize the Resources starting year.
- Defer Privacy, Terms, and Cookies pages for now.

## Consequences
- Resources must use an explicit/stable exam ordering and must not alphabetically sort `paperTag` values.
- `PRD.md` section 8 records these settled decisions; R5 implementation remains pending.
- Legal-page work is out of scope for now. Logged-out footer navigation remains a separate issue.
- This decision supersedes the Programming homepage conflict recorded in `decisions/5.md`.
